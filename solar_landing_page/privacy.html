Zero-Data-Sharing Guarantee

Solops executes an isolated tenant architecture. Customer leads, site survey media, DISCOM feasibility applications, and 70:30 GST financial records stored in your company workspace (e.g. yourcompany.solops.cloud) are accessible exclusively to your authorized personnel. Digiink Solutions LLP never aggregates, monetizes, or sells tenant data to competitors or third parties.

1. Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy outlines how Digiink Solutions LLP ("Digiink", "Company", "we", "our", or "us"), an Indian Limited Liability Partnership registered in Jaipur, Rajasthan, collects, processes, secures, and handles operational and personal data via our enterprise multi-tenant Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) application Solops accessible at https://solops.cloud and dedicated tenant subdomains https://*.solops.cloud.

This policy applies to all registered solar installation businesses ("Tenants", "Clients"), their designated Super Administrators, Operations Managers, Sales Representatives, Site Survey Engineers, and any prospective customers who submit inquiries via our public website.

Our data protection practices strictly adhere to the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), the Information Technology Act, 2000, and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011.

2. Information We Collect

We process information categorized into Tenant Account Data, Solar Operations Data, and Platform Telemetry:

A. Tenant Business & Administrative Information

  • Company Registration Details: Legal entity name, GSTIN, registered corporate address, official state of operations, and business contact numbers.
  • Authorized Personnel Profiles: Super Admin and employee full names, corporate email addresses, phone numbers, assigned roles, and login credentials (stored using salted cryptographic hashes).
  • Billing & Subscription Records: Plan tier, payment transaction identifiers, payment gateway invoice receipts, and GST billing dossiers.

B. Solar Operations & Customer Data (Entered by Tenants)

As a multi-tenant CRM, Solops enables tenants to store and manage residential, commercial, and industrial rooftop solar projects:

  • Solar Leads & Customers: Customer name, contact number, installation site address, pin code, state, city, and GPS coordinates.
  • Technical Specifications: Electricity DISCOM consumer connection number (CA Number), sanctioned sanction load, sanctioned kW capacity, proposed solar module models, and inverter serial numbers.
  • DISCOM & Subsidy Documentation: PM Surya Ghar registration IDs, feasibility approval letters, net-metering test reports, and inspection clearances.
  • Site Inspection Media: Rooftop photographs, shadow analysis snapshots, structural drawings, and installation completion handover dossiers.
  • Commercial Invoices: Quotations, 70:30 GST compliant invoices, milestone advance payments, and customer balance receipts.

C. Automatically Collected Technical Metadata

  • Access IP addresses, browser specifications, operating system versions, and time-stamped system activity audit logs to prevent unauthorized penetration or credential abuse.

3. Multi-Tenant Database Isolation Architecture

Solops is engineered from the ground up for strict enterprise isolation. Unlike legacy single-database multi-tenant systems where multiple companies share the same table rows, Solops implements complete database-level separation:

Isolated Database Schemas

Each tenant is provisioned an independent MySQL database (e.g. db_client_001). No joint table sharing exists.

Tenant Subdomain Guard

Requests to yourcompany.solops.cloud verify host headers dynamically. Cross-tenant data leakage is cryptographically and logically prohibited.

4. How We Use Tenant Data

We process collected data exclusively for the following operational purposes:

  1. Platform Operation: Provisioning tenant subdomains, running CRM modules, executing solar capacity calculations, generating system quotations, and tracking follow-ups.
  2. Compliance & Invoicing: Generating accurate GST tax invoices under HSN/SAC codes for solar products and services.
  3. System Notifications: Sending automated transactional email alerts (password reset links, subscription receipts, critical follow-up reminders).
  4. Platform Reliability & Security: Detecting unauthorized intrusions, preventing brute-force login attempts, and maintaining 99.9% uptime.

5. Data Retention & Tenant Ownership

You own 100% of your business data. Digiink Solutions LLP asserts no ownership rights over customer leads, survey media, or billing invoices entered into your workspace.

  • Active Subscriptions: Data remains stored securely in your dedicated database for the duration of your active subscription.
  • Account Deactivation / Cancellation: Upon subscription expiration or termination, your data is retained in a dormant, read-only state for thirty (30) calendar days to permit full data export. After 30 days, tenant databases and attached media are permanently and irreversibly purged from our production servers upon formal request.
  • Automated Backups: Encrypted backup snapshots are retained in secondary cold storage for ninety (90) days for disaster recovery purposes before rolling deletion.

6. Information Security Standards

We deploy rigorous technical and procedural security controls:

  • Encryption in Transit: All HTTP traffic across solops.cloud and tenant subdomains is strictly enforced over TLS 1.3/SSL with 256-bit encryption.
  • Password Security: Passwords are cryptographically hashed using industry-standard Blowfish algorithms (bcrypt) with random salting. Cleartext passwords are never stored or logged.
  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Granular permissions restrict sensitive modules (financials, employee management, deletions) strictly to designated roles (Super Admin, Manager, Sales, Engineer).
  • Infrastructure Hardening: Production servers operate behind enterprise firewalls with automated rate limiting, intrusion detection, and strict privilege isolation.

7. Third-Party Integrations & Sub-processors

To deliver specific platform capabilities, Solops interfaces with select enterprise service providers bound by strict confidentiality and data protection agreements:

  • Cloud Infrastructure Providers: ISO/IEC 27001 certified data center facilities for secure hosting.
  • Transactional SMTP Providers: For delivering system activation, verification, and reset emails.
  • Payment Gateways (Razorpay / Cashfree): For processing subscription payments. Credit card and net-banking credentials are processed directly by RBI-authorized payment aggregators; Solops never stores card numbers.

8. Tenant Rights & Data Portability

Under the DPDP Act 2023, administrators have comprehensive rights regarding their business data:

  • Right to Access: View all stored records directly via CRM workspace tables.
  • Right to Rectification: Update, edit, or correct any client, project, or employee record in real-time.
  • Right to Data Portability: Export lead databases, project timelines, and customer rosters into standard Microsoft Excel (.xlsx) or CSV formats at any time.
  • Right to Erasure: Request permanent destruction of tenant databases and associated media files upon contract completion.

9. Cookies & Session Management

Solops utilizes strictly functional HTTP cookies and Local Storage session tokens essential for maintaining authenticated user sessions across the web portal. We do not use third-party cross-site advertising or behavioural tracking cookies.
