This notice explains how Digiink Solutions LLP operates SolOps and handles information provided through this website, trial registration and hosted CRM workspaces. Contact us at digiink2505@gmail.com or +91 90241 54995 for privacy questions or requests.

1. Information we collect

  • Trial registration: business name, contact person, email address, phone number, optional city/state and chosen workspace name.
  • Verification and account records: email verification code hashes, expiry and attempt records, registration time, Terms/Privacy version and acceptance time, account and subscription details.
  • Technical records: request/network information needed to operate and secure the service. The trial service stores a hashed network identifier for rate limiting; infrastructure logs may contain IP addresses.
  • Workspace data: records that your organisation chooses to enter, such as leads, customers, project documents, employee assignments and billing records. Tenant administrators control how their team uses these records.

2. Why we use it

We use registration information to verify your email, create and support your workspace, communicate login and service information, manage subscriptions, prevent abuse and respond to requests. Signing up does not subscribe you to unrelated marketing. Transactional account, security and service emails are part of operating your account.

3. Workspace data and your responsibilities

Each company is assigned its own workspace and database. Authorised team members access data according to their permissions; authorised service personnel may access information when necessary for support, operation or security. Database separation is not a promise that no authorised support access is possible. Your organisation must have the necessary authority and notices before uploading personal information or documents about its customers or staff.

4. Service providers and integrations

Hosting, email delivery and other service providers process information as needed to operate SolOps. The website may load external fonts, icons or scripts, which can expose normal browser/request metadata to their providers. Integrations such as messaging or advertising require separate configuration and may be subject to the provider?s terms. We do not sell your CRM records. We may disclose information where required by law or to address security incidents.

5. Storage and retention

Verification codes expire after 10 minutes and are stored as hashes. Registration, consent and abuse-prevention metadata may be retained as necessary for account administration, security and legal requirements. Trial workspace records are not deleted immediately on day seven. Under the current subscription lifecycle, unrenewed workspaces may be scheduled for deletion 20 days after subscription expiry; the account notice and master-admin arrangements determine the applicable date. Contact us before expiry if you need help retaining or exporting records. Deleted active data can remain in operational backups until those copies rotate or are removed under applicable requirements.

6. Security

We use measures including HTTPS connections, password hashing, email verification and access controls. No online system can guarantee absolute security. Keep your credentials confidential, change temporary passwords after login and inform us promptly about suspected misuse.

7. Your choices and requests

You may contact us to request access, correction, export or deletion of your information, withdraw consent where applicable, or raise a grievance. We may verify your identity and authority first. Withdrawing information needed to operate a workspace may mean we cannot continue providing the service. Retention obligations, security records and rights of other users may affect a request. For records held by a solar business using SolOps, contact that business first; we can assist its authorised administrator.

8. Browser storage and children

The CRM uses browser storage to maintain sessions and preferences. Trial registration is for authorised business representatives aged 18 or older. SolOps is not intended for children. Contact us if a child's information was submitted in error.

9. Updates and privacy contact

We may update this notice as the service changes. The current version and date appear above. New trial registrations record the version acknowledged. Send privacy and grievance requests to digiink2505@gmail.com, with "SolOps Privacy" in the subject. You can also call +91 90241 54995.
