Comprehensive Solar EPC Capabilities

Built to cover every technical, regulatory, and commercial milestone in the Indian solar lifecycle.

Lead Management

Inbound lead routing, follow-up scheduling, and stage progression without duplicate records.

Solar Quotation Calculator

Compute module count, inverter sizing, estimated solar generation, and customer payback period.

Customer Management

Maintain electricity bills, sanctioned load documents, DISCOM account details, and site history.

Site Surveys

Record roof tilt, azimuth, parapet shadows, cable distances, and single vs three phase availability.

70:30 GST Billing Workflow

Automate statutory 70% Goods (5% GST) and 30% Services (18% GST) split billing with milestone receipts.

PM Surya Ghar Subsidy Tracking

Track National Portal ARN progress from Feasibility Approval to DISCOM net-meter installation and DBT release.

Jan Samarth Loan Pipeline

Track bank loans across 5 structured stages: Eligibility, Applied, Review, Sanctioned, and Disbursed.

Inventory Management

Track stock levels for modules, inverters, rails, and DC cables with stock-in, stock-out, and barcode scanning.

Installation Tracking

Monitor physical mounting, cable clamping, inverter earthing, and Megger insulation resistance testing.

Field Engineer Operations

Assign field crews, manage site visits on mobile browsers, and collect customer verification signatures.

Commercial Payment Ledger

Enforce milestone payment receipts and handover locks before release of official commissioning documents.

Warranty and AMC Management

Track recurring Annual Maintenance Contracts, scheduled module washings, and equipment replacement warranties.
