What is SolOps solar CRM software?

SolOps is cloud-based solar business management software from Digiink Solutions LLP. It connects leads, customer records, quotations, invoices, stock, project delivery and after-sales work in a company workspace.

Who is SolOps built for?

It is designed for solar installers and EPC businesses managing residential rooftop and commercial solar projects. Six web portals support Admin, Manager, Sales, Site Engineer, Finance and Application Manager workflows.

How do I start the 7-day free trial?

Open Start free trial, enter your business and contact details, choose your workspace name and accept the Terms and Privacy Policy. Verify the code sent to your email. Your trial begins when the workspace is created. No payment card is required.

Can my team use SolOps on mobile?

The web workspace works in a mobile browser. A native Android admin app is also being prepared for download. Its current capabilities are described on the Downloads page; the APK download is not yet available.

Does SolOps automatically submit subsidy or loan applications?

No. Your team records application information, references and milestones in the CRM. DISCOM, government portals and banks control their own approvals and processing. A recorded CRM status does not confirm an external approval or payment.

Can I connect Meta, Google Ads or WhatsApp?

Integration settings are available for Meta Lead Ads, Google Ads lead forms and WhatsApp Business API. They require your business accounts, credentials and provider configuration before use. Contact us to discuss setup.

What happens when the free trial ends?

Trial access expires after 7 days. Data is not immediately deleted at expiry. Contact the SolOps team to arrange a paid subscription and review the Terms and Privacy Policy for retention details.

Do I need to enter card details or select a paid plan?

No card details are required for the 7-day trial. The trial option is fixed during registration. Contact +91 90241 54995 or digiink2505@gmail.com to discuss a paid plan for your business.
