GOOD TO KNOW

A few quick answers.

Can I use my existing SolOps account?

Yes. Enter your company workspace and use your existing credentials. Ask your company administrator if you need an account or help finding your workspace.

Will the app and website use the same data?

The app connects to your company's hosted SolOps CRM. Records saved successfully to the server can be accessed from the web and mobile workspace, subject to your permissions.

Is an iPhone download available?

This page is for the Android app. An iOS download is not currently available here. You can continue using your company workspace in a browser.

Do I still need the web workspace?

Yes. Use the web workspace for tasks such as quotation creation and editing, standalone invoice creation, reports and bulk imports.
