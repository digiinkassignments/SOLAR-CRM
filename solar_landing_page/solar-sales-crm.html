THE SOLOPS PLATFORM

Keep solar sales moving, one clear next action at a time.

Solar sales takes more than a contact list. Teams need the requirement, lead source, owner, conversation history and next action in the same place. SolOps supports this through a sales workspace connected to customer and project operations.

Capture the requirement and the context

Record the customer contact, lead source and solar requirement so the next person handling the lead understands the conversation. Lead assignment gives the salesperson a clear working list and lets the manager review the leads handled by their reporting team.

Use customer notes for site instructions and relevant preferences rather than relying on details that only exist in a personal chat.

Make every follow-up useful

A useful follow-up has a purpose, a date and a time. Logging the outcome helps the salesperson decide whether to schedule another conversation, arrange a visit or update the lead's status.

  • Keep the latest conversation and outcome alongside the lead.
  • Plan the next action and follow-up schedule.
  • Review the day's follow-up work in the sales workspace.
  • Keep customer contact and assignment details current.

Reminder delivery depends on the configured service and account settings. A saved follow-up is a task record, not a guarantee that a message was delivered.

Follow a pipeline that reflects the conversation

The lead lifecycle includes New Lead, Contacted, Qualified, Site Survey Scheduled, Site Survey Done, Quotation Sent, Negotiation, Won, On Hold, Lost and Invalid.

Choose the status that reflects the actual customer situation. A status update should help the team understand what happened and what to do next; it should not replace the notes or evidence needed for a project handoff.

Coordinate the site survey

When the lead is ready for a visit, schedule the engineer and provide the site address, landmark and location details available for that customer. A useful site brief reduces time spent calling for directions.

Survey findings and normal uploaded photographs give the team context for the proposed system and subsequent work. The current web workflow supports ordinary uploaded photos; automatic photo geotagging is not a promised capability.

After the engineer marks the survey feasible, the configured workflow creates the customer and project without an additional survey approval step.

Carry the conversation into the commercial handoff

Once the requirement is sufficiently clear, prepare the proposal from the web workspace. Connect the customer details, system information, commercial amount and supporting notes so the delivery team has context for what was discussed.

Projects provide a separate workspace for installation, commissioning and handover. Sales, collections and delivery should stay connected without forcing every business activity into one single status field.

Give managers a team view

Salespeople work their assigned leads. Managers coordinate the salespeople reporting to them and review the relevant team's lead activity. Admin users oversee company operations through the wider workspace.
