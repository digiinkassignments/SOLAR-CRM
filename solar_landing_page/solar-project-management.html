THE SOLOPS PLATFORM

Keep the work behind every solar installation connected.

Solar project management connects site information, assignments, material readiness, field updates, commissioning and customer handover. SolOps gives your team a project workspace alongside the customer and commercial records.

Start with the site, not just the capacity

A project begins with a real location and a customer requirement. The survey provides site observations, practical access information and the documents your team needs for the next step.

Capture the address, landmark and available location details for the visiting engineer. Upload ordinary site photographs and the required customer documents in the survey workflow. Automatic geotagging is not part of the current photo-upload promise.

When the engineer records a feasible survey, the configured workflow creates the customer and project without an additional approval step.

Give delivery a clear starting point

Keep system capacity, customer details, project ownership, relevant dates and project notes visible. The customer, quotation and invoice context helps the delivery team understand the commercial scope and collection position.

Material and scheduling work often runs alongside external utility and finance processes. Treat these as related workstreams rather than assuming every project follows the same timing.

Record the installation as it happens

Engineers can access assigned projects, review work and record installation progress. Work records and uploaded evidence provide a clearer account of the site than an unstructured stream of messages.

  • Keep assigned work and project context accessible.
  • Record progress and field observations.
  • Capture commissioning and inspection information.
  • Keep stage history available for the wider team.

Record actual work truthfully. A saved stage is a CRM record and does not by itself establish that an external inspection or approval has occurred.

Make customer handover understandable

Handover includes more than customer acceptance. Training, monitoring access, documents, payment clearance and the actual completion information may all matter to the business workflow.

SolOps keeps customer acceptance separate from overall handover completion so a recorded acceptance is not presented as proof that every other condition has been met. Supporting PDF or photo actions are shown when the relevant links exist.

Keep warranty and AMC work connected

Record component warranty information and service details in the project workspace. Companies can configure AMC plans around their own service offering instead of relying on a fixed Basic, Standard or Premium catalogue.

Use service visits, contract dates and renewal information as working records for the after-sales team. Record the terms actually agreed with the customer and review the appropriate invoice and payment workflow with finance.

Understand the parallel application work

Subsidy, net-metering and lender milestones do not always progress at the same pace as physical installation. Track references and recorded milestones in Subsidy & Finance while the team manages project delivery.

DISCOMs, government portals and lenders control their own processing. SolOps tracking does not replace their official portals or guarantee their approvals, schedules or payments.

Connect the office and the site

The web platform offers focused Admin, Manager, Sales, Site Engineer, Finance and Application Manager portals. The Android admin companion brings selected business records to mobile; see available app capabilities and download availability.
