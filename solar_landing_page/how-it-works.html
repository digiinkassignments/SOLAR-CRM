REGISTER. VERIFY. GET TO WORK.

Your company workspace, in a few clear steps.

Register your solar business, verify your email and explore SolOps with a 7-day free trial. No payment card is required, and your trial starts when the workspace is created.

Start a 7-day free trial Explore the modules

1. Verify your email first

Open Start free trial. Enter your email and request a six-digit code. Verify the code to unlock the business form. Codes expire in 10 minutes; allow 60 seconds before resending. A verified session lasts 30 minutes.

Use an email inbox you can access. The verification code and workspace information are sent there. If your details are already registered, contact support rather than creating duplicate records.

2. Add business details and your workspace address

Enter your business name, contact person and Indian mobile number; city and state are optional. Your verified email stays locked. Changing it requires verification again. The form suggests a workspace name from your business name. You can edit it before continuing. For example, a workspace named sunrisesolar uses the address sunrisesolar.solops.cloud.

The workspace name uses 4-30 lowercase letters, numbers or hyphens, starts with a letter and ends with a letter or number. Reserved or already-used names cannot be registered. The registration plan stays fixed as a 7-day free trial.

3. Review the policies and create your workspace

Read the Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy, then tick the required consent box. The subscription is a fixed 7-day free trial. Select Create my workspace once and keep the page open while setup finishes.

Repeated requests do not create another workspace. If setup is taking longer than expected, check your email or contact support before registering again.

4. Save your login details and sign in

After successful verification and setup, the page shows your workspace link, username, temporary password and trial end time. Save those details securely. A welcome email is also attempted; the page reports if it could not be sent.

Open your workspace and sign in. Change the temporary password when prompted. Do not share the administrator password with the whole team; create individual user accounts for day-to-day work.

5. Set up your company and team

Review company settings and branding first. Add the relevant team accounts and responsibilities, then enter a lead or customer to explore the workflow.

  • Sales: record a lead, next action and follow-up.
  • Engineering: review an assigned survey or project.
  • Finance: explore customer records, quotations and invoices.
  • Applications: review subsidy and loan tracking.
  • Admin and Manager: coordinate company and team work.

Use accurate customer information and only upload documents you are authorised to handle.

6. Understand web and app access

The full web workspace is available in a browser. Quotation creation and editing, standalone invoices, reports and bulk imports are web workflows.

The Android admin app supports selected mobile workflows described on Downloads. The APK download is not yet available; the website will show the official link when the release is ready.

7. Decide what works for your business

Use the trial to explore your own business workflows and team responsibilities. Trial access expires after 7 days; data is not immediately deleted at the moment of expiry. Review the current Terms and Privacy Policy for retention information.
