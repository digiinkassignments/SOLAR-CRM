2. Description of Services & Dedicated Subdomain Allocation

Solops provides specialized cloud-hosted software modules designed for Indian residential, commercial, and industrial rooftop solar installation companies:

  • Tenant Subdomain Provisioning: Each verified client is allocated a dedicated private workspace URL under the Solops domain namespace (e.g. https://yourcompany.solops.cloud) secured with automated SSL wildcard encryption.
  • CRM & Pipeline Management: Inbound lead management, automated follow-up scheduling, birthday/anniversary event alerts, and status pipeline stages.
  • Technical Field Operations: Mobile-responsive site assessment forms, rooftop shading capture, structural load records, and installation progress tracking.
  • DISCOM & Subsidy Documentation: PM Surya Ghar tracking, consumer application feasibility milestones, and net-metering commissioning checklists.
  • 70:30 GST Financial Dossiers: Milestone invoice generation, GST tax split computation (70% goods @ relevant rate, 30% services @ 18%), and quotation generation.

3. 7-Day Free Trial, Subscription Plans & Invoicing

A. 7-Day Unrestricted Free Trial

Qualified solar EPC companies are eligible for an unrestricted 7-day trial period upon approval of their trial application. During the trial period, the tenant receives full access to the dedicated subdomain and core software modules. No payment method is required to initiate the trial.

B. Subscription Activation & Billing

Prior to or upon the conclusion of the 7-day trial, the tenant may select an operational tier (Monthly, Quarterly, or Annual subscription). Services continue uninterrupted upon timely settlement of subscription charges. All fees are exclusive of applicable Indian Goods and Services Tax (GST @ 18%), which will be charged on official tax invoices.

C. Non-Refundable Nature of Subscriptions

Due to cloud infrastructure provisioning costs and dedicated database allocation, all subscription payments are non-refundable once processed, except where explicitly mandated by applicable statutory Indian law.

4. User Roles, Access Control & Tenant Account Security

Solops provides 4 structured employee roles to enforce organizational discipline:

  1. Super Admin: Full administrative rights, financial overview, user provisioning, role assignments, and client configuration.
  2. Operations Manager: Lead distribution, project scheduling, milestone approvals, and survey reviews.
  3. Sales Executive: Customer follow-ups, quotation creation, and pipeline execution.
  4. Field Engineer: Rooftop inspection, mobile site uploads, and technical verification checklists.

The Tenant Super Administrator is solely responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of all employee login credentials. Any activity executed under tenant account credentials shall be deemed the direct responsibility of the tenant organization.

7. Solar Calculations & Regulatory Verification Disclaimer

Solops incorporates automated solar generation estimators, capacity sizing calculators, and 70:30 GST invoice formulas designed to accelerate EPC quoting.

While our formulas reflect standard industry benchmarks and prevailing Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE) guidelines, the Tenant's qualified technical engineers remain solely responsible for validating physical rooftop structural feasibility, shadow profiles, localized DISCOM net-metering norms, and certified electrical drawings prior to physical procurement and installation.
