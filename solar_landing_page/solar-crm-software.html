What should a solar CRM help you manage?

A solar business works across several parallel responsibilities: qualifying a customer, surveying the roof, proposing a system, arranging materials, installing it, collecting payments and supporting it after handover. A useful CRM keeps the customer context connected across these activities.

SolOps provides a dedicated company workspace with six web portals: Admin, Manager, Sales, Site Engineer, Finance and Application Manager. The visible records and actions depend on the role and configuration.

Customer records with commercial context

A customer profile includes contact information, installation address, customer type, lead source and utility details. Plant size, final amount and payment received can be recorded as an opening record when bringing existing customers into the system.

  • Keep the site address, city, state and electricity connection details together.
  • Review linked projects, quotations, invoices and lead history.
  • Edit opening amounts, payment dates, modes and references where applicable.
  • Bring customer information in through the web bulk-import workflow.

An opening amount is not a generated tax invoice. Avoid entering the same commercial transaction both as an opening record and a new invoice.

Quotations, invoices and recorded payments

Use the web workspace to prepare quotations with system and customer details, line items, stock selection and a tax summary. Company information and branding support consistent customer-facing documents.

Invoice records help teams review the commercial amount, recorded payments and balance due. Keep receipts and payment references clear, and review the appropriate tax treatment for each actual contract with your finance adviser.

Quotation creation and editing, standalone invoice creation and bulk imports are web workflows. The Android app is a companion to the web platform, with its currently available capabilities described on the Downloads page.

Inventory and material visibility

Track solar stock items, stock movements, transaction history, shortage alerts and valuation. Inventory visibility helps your team prepare for a planned installation and follow up on missing materials.

A stock record alone does not establish that material has been installed at a site. Keep project work records and actual material movements consistent.

Subsidy and loan tracking

The Subsidy & Finance workspace records application references, sanctioned capacity, portal milestones, loan information and recorded disbursements. This gives the responsible team a clear follow-up context.

DISCOM, government and lender processes remain external. Updating a CRM milestone does not submit a portal application, approve a loan or confirm that subsidy money has reached the customer.

Reports, team access and company configuration

Use the web platform to review reports and manage company settings, team accounts, branding and operational configuration. Managers work with their reporting team; salespeople and engineers have focused responsibilities around their assigned work.

Provider integration settings are available for Meta Lead Ads, Google Ads lead forms and WhatsApp Business API. Actual use requires your business accounts, credentials, configuration and provider testing.
